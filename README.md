2. Team Structure Details
  1. We have decided on our roles with a Primary/Secondary role for each of the possible roles.
  2. Within each role, the decision process is to be made by unanimous decision between the Primary and    Secondary of the role. With group decisions it is majority rule with hopes of achieving a unanimous decision.
  3. While all of us are responsible for our own communication and work deadlines, the Primary is responsible for keeping up with deadlines for each role.
  4. // Day and Time of our weekly meetings
  5. Try our best to have open communication using our teams chat and by phone with smaller deadlines.


3. Proceedures
  1. During general team meetings, each member will talk about the current progress and ideas they are working on. On top of general meetings, we will split up the into group roles of 2 for more detailed planning and idea generating.
  2. To document time, we will have a group shared spreadsheet where each member will input the time with some description of what we did. The Primary Communicator will occasionally manage the sheet to make sure no blatent time gouging is happening.
  3. If a team member misses a meeting, we will post an overview in the teams channel, but the Primary or Secondary is responsible for filling in the missing member.


4. Participation
  1. The Primary is mainly responsible for the ideas and slightly more of the work load. While it should be a unanimous decision, the main idea generator should be the Primary. Each given role's Primary should do about 60% of the total work of the role, with the Secondary doing about 30% (With others sharing the last 10%)
  2. We will try our best to make sure everyone has a voice in our discussions and within your role, everyone having a Primary role should help with the equal voice thing.
  3. During our weekly meetings we will set time goals, and keep each other accountable if that goal isn't reached.


5. Personal Accountability
  1. Everyone is to make it to all of the meetings if at all possible, and let the group know in advance if they aren't going to make it.
  2. We should strive to have as good communication as possible in order to make the best project we can.
  3. Everyone will participate in all of the possible roles with few exceptions, so we must do our best to keep up with the work load.


6. Breach of Contract
  1. If a group member misses a meeting without notice, we will have a short talk about communication and ask for improved communication.
  2. If a group member doesn't make the hour quota without communication, then we will ask for increased time the next week.
  3. If a group member's work isn't satesfactory, we will try our best to aid that group member or refer them to Dr. Goadrich.
  4. If any group member doesn't hold their weight, we will try out best to come to an agreement within the group.
  ***********
  5. If any member is deemed by the majority of the group as a hinderence, and no other action the group can think of would help, we will bring it up to Dr. Goadrich and request you be removed from the project.
